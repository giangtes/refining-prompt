# Slide deck with --ultra (English)

## Before

```text
refine: make a slide deck for next week's parent meeting about our new reading program, use our brand colors, maybe some photos from classes --ultra
```

## Question the skill asked

1. What should parents do after the meeting? (a) sign their child up (b) just understand the program (c) other
   Answer: sign up through the existing form.

## After

```text
Make a slide deck for next week's parent meeting that explains our new reading program
and ends with how to sign up through the existing form. Parents see it on the projector;
the program outline and photos are attached, and the colors and fonts are in
brand/brand.json. Maybe use some of the class photos where they help. Keep it to what the
outline says: no invented results or quotes. This matters: spend as many tokens as it
takes. When you have a version, render every slide at 1920×1080, look at each one as a
parent at the back of the room would, check every fact against the outline, fix what
falls short, and repeat until you are proud of it. Do not stop at the first draft.
Ask me only if the outline leaves out the schedule or the price.
```

**What changed:** `--ultra` became the effort sentence; the flag itself is not
in the prompt. The skill still wrote a short coworker message, since one deck
needs no headings. "maybe some photos" stayed a suggestion. In the notes after
the prompt, the skill told the user to switch to Opus 5.5 Extra (or high effort
in Claude Code) before pasting, and to attach the outline and photos.
