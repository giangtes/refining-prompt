# Compliance research (English)

## Before

```text
improve my prompt: check if our center posting student photos on facebook is legal
```

## Questions the skill asked

1. What will you do with the result? Answer in one sentence.
2. Which country's law applies?
3. Who will read the result: only you, your staff, or an outside lawyer?
4. What exactly do you post, and how old are the students?

## After

```text
Check whether our English center in Vietnam may lawfully post class photos and short
videos of students on the center's Facebook page, and draft the consent form parents must
sign. The director and admin staff will use the result to decide whether to keep posting.

Context:
- Students are 10–15, so every student is a minor. Vietnamese law only.
- Start from the Civil Code 2015 (right to one's image), the Law on Children 2016 and the
  personal data protection rules, but confirm each one is in force today and find any
  text that replaced it.
- When sources disagree, the primary legal text wins over any summary, and a newer text
  wins over an older one.

Done means:
- A short answer first: may we keep posting, and under what conditions.
- Every legal claim cites a primary source with article number and date.
- A table: requirement, what it asks of us, which ages it covers, source.
- A one-page consent form in Vietnamese with separate tick boxes for photos, videos and
  names, and a withdrawal clause.
- A short checklist the admin staff can follow before each post.

Avoid:
- Blogs or law-firm articles as the only source for a legal claim.
- Presenting a draft law as already in force.
- Legal-advice phrasing; list open questions for a lawyer instead.

Stop and ask me only if no primary source exists for a requirement that looks material.
Otherwise keep going.

Finish with three headings: Blocked on me, Changed, Found.
Mark anything you could not confirm and say where you looked.
```

**What changed:** "is it legal" became a decision, a reader, sourced
requirements and a usable consent form; the brief tells the model to verify
which laws are current instead of trusting a list.
